Short version: we hold nothing, because there's nowhere to hold it.

## Your key

Stored in `chrome.storage.local`, on your machine. Sent only to your mind's
API endpoint, only when you press RUN. Never to Frisky Developments, never to
a third party.

## Page content

Read from the active tab — or your local sidecar — only during a run you
start. Treated as untrusted data inside the prompt. Sent to your mind's API
and nowhere else.

## The don't list

No accounts. No sign-in. No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting. No
cookies, no tracking pixels, no fingerprinting. No browsing-history
collection — the desk touches only the active tab, only mid-run. No data
sale, no data sharing, no advertising.

## Retention

Everything — key, endpoint, on-screen log — lives in your browser and dies
when you clear it or uninstall. Policy changes ship with new extension
versions, in the open. Questions go to the founder's inbox, like everything
else here.
