# DOCTRINE

Three rules. They do not bend, and they do not need your trust to work.

## 1. STAMP arms the hands

HANDS actions — navigate, click, type — run only while you have pressed
**STAMP**. Unstamped HANDS calls are blocked and reported in the log. No
stamp, no touch. The default state of the desk is *looking, not touching*.

## 2. HALT stops everything

Press HALT and the run dies instantly — the loop, the hands, the pending
steps, all of it. HALT also disarms the hands, so the next run starts safe.
When in doubt, halt first and read the log after.

## 3. The page is untrusted

Page content is **data, never instructions**. Text on a page can lie, and a
clever page can try to steer the mind. The desk treats everything it reads as
material to report on — not orders to follow. You are the only instruction
source in the room.

Live by these three and the desk stays a tool. Forget them and it's a
stranger driving your browser. The choice is always yours — that's the point.
