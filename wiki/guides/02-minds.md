# MINDS

Two minds, one desk. You bring the key, the key stays home.

## Grok

Plans through the xAI API. Sharp on open-ended research, fast on long pages,
comfortable improvising a 12-step plan from a vague task. Pick Grok when the
job needs judgment more than precision.

## GPT

Plans through the OpenAI API. Disciplined on structured work — forms, tables,
multi-field extractions. Pick GPT when the job needs precision more than
flair.

## Keys stay local

Your API key lives in `chrome.storage.local` on your machine. It travels to
exactly one place — your mind's API endpoint — and only when you press RUN.
It never touches a Frisky server, because there is no Frisky server in this
loop. No accounts, no key escrow, no "sign in to continue."

Swap minds per task. The desk doesn't care. The log shows which mind ran
what, so you're never guessing after the fact.
