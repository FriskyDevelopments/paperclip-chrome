Sideload today — 60 seconds
  1. Grab the source: github.com/FriskyDevelopments/paperclip-chrome — clone it or download the zip and unzip.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and flip Developer mode on (top right).
  3. Hit Load unpacked and pick the repo's extension/ folder.
  4. Pin FR!sky Paperclip, open the side panel, and you're at the desk.
