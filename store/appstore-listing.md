## Description

FR!sky Paperclip is the agent desk for your Mac's menu bar.

Not a browser tab. Not a grok.com bookmark. A native menu-bar app: press ⌥⌘P from
anywhere — any Space, any full-screen app — and the desk floats in, ready.

HOW IT WORKS
• Type a task. Grok (grok-4-fast via the xAI API) plans and acts in up to 12 steps.
• Clip can read the active tab and snapshot the page for free.
• HANDS actions — navigate, click, type — only run while you've pressed STAMP.
• Press HALT and everything stops, instantly. Halt also disarms the hands.
• Page content is treated as untrusted data, never as instructions.

NATIVE TO THE MAC
• Lives in the menu bar. No Dock icon, no clutter.
• Global hotkey ⌥⌘P toggles the desk from anywhere.
• Floating panel that joins every Space, even over full-screen apps.
• Native STAMP and HALT buttons in a doctrine-styled toolbar.
• The whole desk ships inside the app — it opens instantly, online or off.

SAFETY BY DESIGN
• Stamp arms Clip's hands. Halt stops everything.
• Unstamped HANDS calls are blocked and reported in the log.
• Max 12 steps per run — the loop can't spin forever.
• Every step, tool call, and result is visible in the desk log.

YOUR KEY, YOUR MACHINE
• Bring your own xAI API key. It stays on this Mac.
• Page content goes to api.x.ai only when you press RUN.
• No accounts. No analytics. Nothing sent to Frisky servers.
